Yes! University search, scholarship finder, and AI chat are all free. The AI uses Gemini — bring your own free key from Google AI Studio.

It uses Google Gemini 2.0 Flash configured as a university admissions expert. It remembers your conversation and replies in your language.

USA, UK, Canada, Europe (Germany, Switzerland, Netherlands), and Asia. 40+ countries with scholarships available globally.
